These drawings are from the WhiteSur icon theme by vinceliuice
(https://github.com/vinceliuice/WhiteSur-icon-theme), licensed GPL-3.0.
Theme.panel() loads them by path for the launcher's power rows.

power-lock.svg, power-kill.svg and power-log-out.svg are our own drawings:
the rounded keyhole (L1), circle close (K6) and person leaving (O3) selected
from the launcher comparison sheet. Their 1-unit outlines match the weight
of the WhiteSur power icons. The launcher uses power-lock.svg separately
from the font glyph used for secured Wi-Fi networks.

launcher.svg is our own drawing of a thin magnifying glass, in an 18px box so it lands on whole pixels. It is pure
white rather than WhiteSur's #ececec: Glyph gives a drawing the label's alpha,
and #ececec under that came out a step dimmer than the text beside it.

steam-tray.svg is panel/22/steam_tray_mono.svg from Themis Icons
(https://github.com/tkutsu/themis-icons), licensed GPL-3.0.
